## The Three Why's — Full Reference

**WHY BUY ANYTHING?**
AI agents are the new unmanaged attack surface. Enterprises are deploying thousands of AI agents — agentic AI, LLM-powered workflows, autonomous decision systems — with no visibility into what identities they carry, what permissions they hold, or who's accountable when one is compromised. Security teams built for human identity are flying blind on machine and AI agent identity.
- 72% of orgs have AI agents in production with no formal identity governance
- AI agents can silently escalate privileges, exfiltrate data, or act as pivot points

**WHY BUY NOW?**
AI agents are already pervasive and growing exponentially. Agentic AI deployment is accelerating. Regulations are catching up — NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act, and emerging SEC requirements are creating accountability for AI agent governance now. Early movers establish governance frameworks before regulators mandate them. Laggards inherit the remediation cost.
- AI agent deployments grew 3x in 2025; 80% of enterprises plan AI agent rollouts by end of 2026
- Average cost of a machine identity-related breach: $4.5M (IBM 2025 Cost of a Data Breach)

**WHY BUY US?**
AppViewX is the only platform with PKI, CLM, and native AI Agent Identity. AI agent identity is a PKI problem. Agents need certificates to authenticate, scope permissions, and enforce expiry. Vendors without PKI and CLM at the core can only deliver partial governance. AppViewX uniquely combines both: battle-tested PKI and CLM trusted by 500 enterprises, and AIS built natively on that PKI foundation. No competitor has both.
- Only vendor unifying enterprise PKI/CLM infrastructure with AI agent Discover → Govern → Secure → Monitor in one workflow
- 500 customers trust AppViewX PKI and CLM today — AIS extends that proven foundation natively to AI agent credentials
