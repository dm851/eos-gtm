02.1 — Prospecting
Signal to Target — the three filters
01 Multi-signal correlation — ZoomInfo, Bombora, LinkedIn, news, job postings weighted together. One signal is noise. Four signals in two weeks is a target.
02 Tech stack scan — BuiltWith or equivalent confirms agent deployment. Copilot, Agentforce, Claude Code, Bedrock, Cursor. If they're running agents, they have the problem.
03 AI-generated account brief — persona, pain, angle, and suggested opening. Delivered to the rep before first touch.
Buying signals to watch
→ Job postings: AI Security Engineer, AI Governance Lead, LLM Infrastructure, Head of AI Risk
→ Earnings call or press release mentioning AI agent deployment or AI governance
→ LinkedIn activity from CISO or VP Engineering around AI security topics
→ Company appears on partner buying list — GuidePoint, Evotec, Carahsoft
02.4 — POV Framework
30-day phase plan
W1 Deploy & Discover — agent inventory, AI bill of materials, initial risk profile
W2 Govern & Assess — posture analysis, compliance mapping (SOC 2, NIST, EU AI Act), mid-point check-in
W3 Secure & Enforce — runtime policies active, MCP gateway configured, lifecycle workflows live
W4 Review & Readout — executive value report, commercial proposal, transition to close
Success criteria
✓ Complete inventory of every AI agent — including previously unknown agents
✓ Audit trail that satisfies SOC 2 auditor requirements
✓ Runtime policies that would have prevented unauthorized agent access
✓ No agent left unowned — lifecycle workflows active
